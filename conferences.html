HKADH 2027 Annual Conference — Model/Making: Toward Generative Humanities

Dates Jan 21–24, 2027
Venue Hong Kong
Organizer Hong Kong Association for Digital Humanities

The annual conference of the Hong Kong Association for Digital Humanities, 21–24 January 2027, exploring models as methods, media, and engines of cultural production.

The Hong Kong Association for Digital Humanities (HKADH) annual conference will take place 21–24 January 2027 under the theme Model/Making: Toward Generative Humanities.

Theme

How have computational models — from statistical corpora to large language models — become central to the way culture is analysed, produced, and circulated? The conference welcomes papers, panels, posters, and demonstrations addressing models as methods, media, and engines of cultural production.

Key dates

  • Abstract deadline: 15 October 2026
  • Notification of acceptance: mid-November 2026
  • Conference dates: 21–24 January 2027

International Conference on Digital Cultural Heritage 2026

Dates Nov 12–14, 2026
Venue Guangzhou, China
Organizer Regional Digital Humanities Consortium

A three-day international conference on digitization, AI, and the preservation of cultural heritage across Asia, 12–14 November 2026.

The International Conference on Digital Cultural Heritage 2026 brings together archivists, librarians, AI researchers, and humanities scholars for three days of exchange on the future of cultural heritage in the digital age.

Themes

  • Digitization pipelines for archives, maps, and manuscripts
  • AI-assisted transcription, translation, and restoration
  • Open data and digital humanities infrastructure
  • Community-centred heritage documentation

Format

  • Keynotes and paper sessions
  • Poster gallery and tool fair
  • Site visits to local heritage digitization labs

Abstract submission closes 15 August 2026. Details are available from the organizing committee.

Conference

The 4th Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities (CDH Summer 2026)

Dates May 13–17, 2026
Venue Online
Organizer Corpora and Digital Humanities Community

An intensive summer college on corpora, NLP, and digital humanities methods, offering lectures and hands-on labs for researchers at all levels.

The 4th Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities is an intensive training event covering corpus construction, computational methods, and their applications in humanities research.

What participants will learn

  • Building and evaluating diachronic and specialized corpora
  • Using NLP tools (tokenization, tagging, parsing) for humanistic text
  • Applying LLMs to corpus research
  • Designing reproducible digital humanities workflows

Format

Lectures in the morning, hands-on labs in the afternoon, and an evening speaker series featuring invited scholars from across Asia and Europe. Suitable for graduate students, faculty, and librarians.
