Student Forum on Chinese Culture in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area

A cross-institutional forum for Chinese cultural studies in the Greater Bay Area, with around thirty to fifty undergraduate and postgraduate students invited and papers in Chinese or English; abstract submissions closed on 15 December 2026 and the forum follows on 9 and 10 January 2027.

Dates
9 and 10 January 2027, tentatively
Place
Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University, Zhuhai
Partners
Division of Humanities, HKUST; Institute for Communication Studies of Chinese Culture and Faculty of Humanities and Social Sciences, BNBU; Faculty of Arts and Humanities, University of Macau
Call issued
24 September 2026
Deadline
15 December 2026, by email to icscc@bnbu.edu.cn, subject Name + University + Paper Title
Submission
A full paper with a 300 to 500 character abstract, original and not under review elsewhere, in Chinese or English
Eligibility
Outstanding undergraduates, master's and doctoral students in Chinese culture fields, including literature, history, philosophy, linguistics, the arts and communication studies, at universities across the Greater Bay Area
Review
Anonymous review by expert reviewers; results expected in late December 2026
Proceedings
Published in Hong Kong, or as a special issue of the Newsletter of the Institute for Communication Studies of Chinese Culture
Costs
The organisers cover meals during the forum; participants cover accommodation and travel

In numbers

  • 30-50students invited
  • 300-500abstract, characters
  • GBAuniversities

Linguistics Futures Forum

A forum in two parts: a language intelligence session on multilingual disinformation detection and on whether models understand sensory experience, then a roundtable on where linguistics stands in the era of large models.

Dates
1 August 2026
Place
Suiyuan Campus, Nanjing Normal University, Nanjing
Partners
HKUST; Nanjing Normal University; University of Macau; Nanjing Agricultural University; BNBU
Finding
Recognition falls markedly in mixed Chinese, English and Russian settings, so cross-lingual generality is not yet achieved
Consensus
Large models are an opportunity rather than a threat; linguistics keeps its own questions while embracing scientific methods

In numbers

  • 48features in the detection model

Themes

  • Multilingual disinformation detection
  • Evaluation standards and disciplinary autonomy
  • Multimodal corpora and a general linguistics toolkit

Selected guests

  • Yuan Wei 原偉 disinformation detection; National University of Defense Technology
  • Long Yunfei 龍雲飛 sensory experience in models; Queen Mary University of London
  • Rao Gaoqi 饒高琦 roundtable moderator; Beijing Language and Culture University
  • Song Rou 宋柔 natural linguistics and humanistic linguistics; Beijing Language and Culture University
  • Tang Xuri 唐旭日 models are tools; Huazhong University of Science and Technology
