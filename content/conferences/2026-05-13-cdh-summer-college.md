The **4th Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities** is an intensive training event covering corpus construction, computational methods, and their applications in humanities research.

## What participants will learn

- Building and evaluating diachronic and specialized corpora
- Using NLP tools (tokenization, tagging, parsing) for humanistic text
- Applying LLMs to corpus research
- Designing reproducible digital humanities workflows

## Format

Lectures in the morning, hands-on labs in the afternoon, and an evening speaker series featuring invited scholars from across Asia and Europe. Suitable for graduate students, faculty, and librarians.

--- 中文 ---

**第四屆語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院**是涵蓋語料庫建設、計算方法及其人文應用的密集培訓活動。

## 學員將學到

- 構建與評估歷時語料庫及專題語料庫
- 運用自然語言處理工具(分詞、標註、句法分析)處理人文文本
- 將大語言模型應用於語料庫研究
- 設計可重現的數碼人文工作流程

## 課程形式

上午講座、下午實作實驗室,並設有邀請亞洲及歐洲學者主講的晚間講座系列。適合研究生、教師及圖書館員參加。
