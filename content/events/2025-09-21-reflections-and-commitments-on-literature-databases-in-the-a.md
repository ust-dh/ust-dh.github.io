Dr. Haitao ZHENG, Lecturer, Anhui Center for Literature Compilation and Research, Huaibei Normal University

Ms. Nan JIA, PhD Student, Department of History, School of History and Archaeology, Nanjing Normal University

The era of virtual intelligence has already arrived, bringing both celebration and criticism. Regarding literature databases, current guidance focuses mainly on technology. We believe that literature should not be reduced to a supporting role; it is both the foundational base and the pillar of the structure. It is not merely a technical object, but also a wellspring of innovation that transcends technology.

--- 中文 ---

南京師範大學潘晟教授以**虛擬智能時代文獻數據庫的反思與堅守**為題，回顧中文文獻數據庫的發展歷程，並討論在人工智能技術快速演進之下，數據庫建設應如何兼顧技術創新與學術嚴謹。
