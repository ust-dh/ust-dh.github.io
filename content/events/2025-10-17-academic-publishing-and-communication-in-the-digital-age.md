Mr. Yang Amin is the chief editor of Erya Culture Publishing House and the editor-in-chief of *Southern Journal of Studies*. His main research areas are classics, literature, and classical texts. He has published works including *Annotations on the Santong* *Xu*, *Chronological Biography of Hu Quan*, and*Interviews with Masters of Classical Literature*, and he has collated and edited *Gongyang Zhuan*. He has published over 40 articles.

--- 中文 ---

楊阿敏先生為爾雅文化出版社總編輯、《南方學刊》主編，研究領域為經學、文學與古典文獻。他以出版實務出發，分享**數碼時代的學術出版與傳播**，包括數碼平台對學術著作流通、評價與閱讀方式的影響。
