This hands-on session offers a **taste of digital scholarship** with Manchu (滿文) materials, tracing the journey of Manchu OCR from **fine-tuned models** to **agentic workflows**.

## Session outline

- Why Manchu OCR is hard: script, layout, and historical context
- Fine-tuning an OCR model on Manchu materials
- From static pipelines to agentic AI workflows
- Live demo and hands-on exercises

**Prerequisites:** no prior experience with Manchu or AI required — curiosity is enough.

Bring your laptop to follow along. Part of the *A Taste of Digital Scholarship* series organized with the HKUST Library.
