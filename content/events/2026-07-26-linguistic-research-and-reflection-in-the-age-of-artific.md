Yuan Yulin, Chair Professor at the University of Macau, reviews how artificial intelligence reshapes linguistic research and reconsiders the place of linguistic theory in the age of AI.

One of the morning lectures of the 4th Summer School on Corpus and Digital Humanities (25 July - 4 August 2026, Nanjing Normal University).

--- 中文 ---

澳門大學人文學院講座教授袁毓林回顧人工智能對語言學研究的衝擊與啟示，討論語言學理論在智能時代的定位與反思。

本講座為第四屆語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院（2026 年 7 月 25 日至 8 月 4 日，南京師範大學）上午講座之一。
