Cameron Campbell, Chair Professor at HKUST, shows how quantitative history and digital humanities support each other, drawing on the China Government Employee Database.

One of the morning lectures of the 4th Summer School on Corpus and Digital Humanities (25 July - 4 August 2026, Nanjing Normal University).

--- 中文 ---

香港科技大學講座教授康文林以清代官員量化資料庫為例，說明量化歷史與數碼人文如何互相支撐歷史研究。

本講座為第四屆語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院（2026 年 7 月 25 日至 8 月 4 日，南京師範大學）上午講座之一。
