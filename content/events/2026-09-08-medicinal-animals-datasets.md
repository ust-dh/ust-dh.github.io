In this seminar, **Liz Chee** introduces *Medicinal Animals and Digital Storytelling*, a platform project that combines historical research with interactive digital storytelling.

## What to expect

- An introduction to the project's historical sources on medicinal animals
- How the team built the underlying **dataset** and enriched it with annotations
- A live walkthrough of the digital storytelling map
- Discussion on the challenges of turning archival material into interactive scholarship

This is a great opportunity for anyone curious about how **datasets power digital humanities storytelling**.

**All are welcome.** No registration required — just come along!
