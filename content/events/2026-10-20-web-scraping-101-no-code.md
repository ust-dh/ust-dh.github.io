Want to collect data from the web for your research but don't know how to code? This hands-on workshop introduces **no-code web scraping tools** that anyone can use.

## Workshop outline

- What web scraping is (and what it isn't)
- Choosing the right no-code tool for common tasks
- Extracting tables, lists, and text from websites
- Saving and cleaning your first dataset
- Ethical and legal considerations

**Prerequisites:** none. Bring your own laptop.

Part of the platform's open training series on digital research methods. Contact **dhi@ust.hk** for enquiries.
