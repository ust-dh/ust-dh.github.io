Learn to turn your research data into compelling visual stories with **Tableau**. This introductory workshop covers the full pipeline from raw data to published dashboard.

## Workshop outline

- Connecting Tableau to spreadsheets and databases
- Building your first charts: bar, line, map, and scatter views
- Designing effective dashboards for research communication
- Publishing and sharing interactive visualizations

**Prerequisites:** a basic familiarity with spreadsheets is helpful. Bring your own laptop and, if you like, a dataset of your own.

Follows the *Web Scraping 101* session on 20 October — sign up for both to build a complete data workflow!

學習使用 **Tableau** 將研究數據轉化為引人入勝的視覺故事。這個入門工作坊涵蓋從原始數據到發佈儀表板的完整流程。

## 工作坊內容

- 將 Tableau 連接至試算表與數據庫
- 建立你的第一個圖表:長條圖、折線圖、地圖與散點圖
- 設計有效的研究傳播儀表板
- 發佈並分享互動視覺化

**參加條件:** 具備基本試算表知識更佳。請自備手提電腦,如有的話亦可自備數據集。

此工作坊緊接10月20日的*《網絡數據擷取入門》* — 同時報名兩場,即可建立完整的數據工作流程!
