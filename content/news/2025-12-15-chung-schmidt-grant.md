**Prof. Michael Chung** has been selected as a **2025 awardee of the Humanities and AI initiative** supported by Schmidt Sciences, co-leading a **US$680,000 research grant**.

## About the grant

The Schmidt Sciences AI-Humanities initiative funds pioneering research that applies artificial intelligence to long-standing questions in the humanities. Prof. Chung's project brings together **AI researchers and humanists** to develop new computational methods for historical text analysis.

## Significance for the platform

This award highlights the growing recognition of digital humanities research at HKUST and strengthens the platform's position at the intersection of **AI and the humanities**. The project will:

- Develop open tools for historical document analysis
- Train students in AI-assisted humanities research
- Foster interdisciplinary collaboration between HKUST units

--- 中文 ---

**鍾衍宏教授**獲選為Schmidt Sciences支持之**「人文與人工智能」計劃2025年度獲獎者**,共同領導一項**68萬美元研究資助**。

## 關於此資助

Schmidt Sciences「人工智能與人文」計劃資助將人工智能應用於人文領域長期問題的開創性研究。鍾教授的項目匯聚**人工智能研究人員與人文學者**,為歷史文本分析開發新的計算方法。

## 對平台的意義

此獎項突顯了科大數碼人文研究日益獲得的認可,亦鞏固了平台在**人工智能與人文**交匯點的地位。項目將:

- 開發歷史文獻分析的開放工具
- 培訓學生進行人工智能輔助的人文研究
- 促進科大各單位之間的跨學科協作
