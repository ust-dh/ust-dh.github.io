**Professor Michael Yan Hon Chung** presented his recent work on **"Agentic AI for Manchu OCR"** at the **BnF Data Lab, Paris** on **16 April 2026**.

## The challenge

Manchu is a Tungusic language with a rich archival tradition, but its unique vertical script makes OCR notoriously difficult. Conventional OCR pipelines struggle with:

- The vertical writing direction of the Manchu script
- Mixed Manchu–Chinese documents
- Degraded quality of 18th–19th century manuscripts

## The approach

The project moves beyond fine-tuned models toward **agentic workflows**, in which AI agents orchestrate multiple tools — segmentation, recognition, post-correction, and evaluation — to progressively refine transcriptions. This builds on the platform's ongoing work on **Manchu OCR and translation AI** for historical archives.

The talk was part of the BnF Data Lab seminar series, connecting digital scholarship communities between Hong Kong and Europe.

**鍾衍宏教授**於**2026年4月16日**在**巴黎法國國家圖書館數據實驗室(BnF Data Lab)**發表了**「滿文OCR智能代理工作流」**的最新研究。

## 挑戰

滿文是擁有豐富檔案傳統的通古斯語系語言,但其獨特的直排文字令OCR格外困難。傳統的OCR流程難以應對:

- 滿文的直排書寫方向
- 滿漢混排的文獻
- 18至19世紀手稿質素欠佳的問題

## 研究方法

此項目超越傳統的微調模型,走向**智能代理工作流(agentic workflows)** — 由人工智能代理協調多種工具,包括文字分割、識別、後期校正與評估,逐步完善轉錄結果。此研究建基於平台在歷史檔案**滿文OCR與翻譯人工智能**方面的持續工作。

是次演講為BnF Data Lab研討會系列的一部分,連繫了香港與歐洲的數碼學術社群。
