A research team at HKUST has achieved the **world's first deep learning application for the Akha language**, an important milestone in using AI to preserve endangered indigenous Southeast Asian languages.

## About the project

Akha is a Tibeto-Burman language spoken by the Akha people across Southeast Asia. With limited written resources and a fast-dwindling number of fluent speakers, the language faces serious preservation challenges.

The project — part of the platform's work on language documentation — applies deep learning models to:

- **Automatic speech recognition** for Akha audio recordings
- **Script conversion** for the community's one-script writing system
- **Machine translation** between Akha, English, and Chinese

## Why it matters

> "Language preservation is not only about archiving — it is about keeping languages alive and usable for future generations."

This work demonstrates how digital humanities methods can directly support cultural heritage communities. The team's approach is designed to be **reusable for other under-resourced languages** across the region.

科大研究團隊為**阿卡語(Akha)**開發了**全球首個深度學習應用**,這是利用人工智能保存瀕危東南亞原住民語言的重要里程碑。

## 關於項目

阿卡語是東南亞阿卡族人使用的藏緬語系語言。由於書面資源有限,加上能流利使用的人口迅速減少,該語言正面臨嚴重的保存挑戰。

此項目 — 屬於平台語言記錄工作的一部分 — 運用深度學習模型實現:

- 阿卡語音頻的**自動語音識別**
- 社區「一體文字」書寫系統的**文字轉換**
- 阿卡語、英語與中文之間的**機器翻譯**

## 為何重要

> 「語言保存不僅是存檔 — 更是讓語言得以延續,並為後代所用。」

此項工作展示了數碼人文方法如何直接支援文化遺產社群。團隊的研究方法設計為可**應用於區內其他資源匱乏的語言**。
