The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), Nanjing Normal University, the University of Macau, Beijing Normal University-Hong Kong Baptist University United International College, and Nanjing Agricultural University recently received recognition for their joint initiative, the "Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities," which was selected as one of the "National Exemplary Cases of Language Technology Innovation Empowering Key Sectors." This initiative stands out as the only language technology summer school to receive this honor, as well as the sole selected cooperative initiative between the Mainland, Hong Kong, and Macau.

The "National Exemplary Cases of Language Technology Innovation Empowering Key Sectors" is the highest honor in China's language technology sector, aiming to commend pivotal projects that make outstanding contributions to the development of key national areas—such as the Digital China Strategy, the "AI Plus" Initiative, and the strategy to build a leading country in education—through language technology. The recognition of this joint initiative fully demonstrates HKUST’s exceptional contributions and profound influence in the fields of language technology, digital humanities, and artificial intelligence.

As the largest and most influential digital humanities summer program in Asia, the "Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities" is hosted annually on a rotating basis among five universities across Hong Kong, Macau, Guangdong, and Jiangsu. This year (2026), the summer college will be held in July at Nanjing Normal University, drawing nearly 2000 applicants from close to 400 universities across 18 countries and regions worldwide. The curriculum for this session features three core thematic modules: corpus programming, linguistic statistical methods, and large language model (LLM) programming. Featured instructors include numerous globally renowned scholars.

Moving forward, the National Language Commission of China and the Department of Language Information Management of the Ministry of Education have pledged their support for the selected initiative. They will also encourage local governments to provide complementary resources, policy support, and promotional backing for the recognized initiatives, thereby driving high-quality development across China's key national areas.

--- 中文 ---

香港科技大學、南京師範大學、澳門大學、北京師範大學—香港浸會大學聯合國際學院及南京農業大學的聯合項目「語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院」，近日入選「語言技術創新賦能重點領域國家典型案例」，是唯一獲此殊榮的語言技術暑期學校，也是唯一入選的內地與港澳合作項目。

「語言技術創新賦能重點領域國家典型案例」為國家語言技術領域最高榮譽，旨在表揚透過語言技術為數字中國戰略、「人工智能+」行動、教育強國建設等國家重點領域發展作出卓越貢獻的關鍵項目。此次入選充分體現科大在語言技術、數碼人文及人工智能領域的卓越貢獻與深遠影響。

「語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院」是亞洲規模最大、最具影響力的數碼人文暑期項目，由香港、澳門、廣東及江蘇五所高校每年輪流主辦。2026 年暑期學院將於七月在南京師範大學舉行，吸引來自全球 18 個國家及地區、近 400 所高校的近 2000 名申請者。本屆課程設三大核心主題模組：語料庫編程、語言統計方法及大型語言模型（LLM）編程，並由多位國際知名學者擔任導師。
