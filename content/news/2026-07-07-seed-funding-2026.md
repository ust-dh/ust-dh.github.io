The Humanities and Digital Technologies Platform is pleased to invite **proposals for interdisciplinary research collaboration in digital humanities for 2026/27**.

## What the funding supports

Seed funding is intended to help faculty members launch or scale up digital humanities projects, including:

- Digitization and curation of archival materials
- Development of digital tools, databases, and platforms
- Collaborative research between humanities scholars and technical experts
- Student involvement in project-based digital scholarship

## Eligibility

- Principal investigators must be HKUST faculty members
- Collaborations across divisions, departments, or with the Library are strongly encouraged
- Projects should demonstrate clear digital humanities methods and outputs

## How to apply

For enquiries, contact **dhi@ust.hk**.

「人文及數碼科技研究平台」欣然邀請提交**2026/27年度數碼人文跨學科研究協作提案**。

## 資助範圍

種子基金旨在協助教師開展或擴展數碼人文項目,包括:

- 檔案材料的數碼化與整理
- 開發數碼工具、數據庫與平台
- 人文學者與技術專家之間的協作研究
- 學生參與以項目為本的數碼學術工作

## 申請資格

- 首席研究員必須為科大教師
- 鼓勵跨學部、跨學系或與圖書館協作
- 項目須展示明確的數碼人文方法與成果

## 如何申請

如有查詢,請聯絡 **dhi@ust.hk**。
