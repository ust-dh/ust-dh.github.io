The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), in collaboration with Nanjing Normal University, the University of Macau, Beijing Normal-Hong Kong Baptist University, and Nanjing Agricultural University, successfully organized the 4th Summer College of Corpora and Digital Humanities (CDH Summer 2026) in Nanjing. The opening ceremony included an address by Prof. LI Ping, Dean of the School of Humanities and Social Science and Chair Professor of the Division of Social Science, and a keynote lecture by Prof. Cameron CAMPBELL, Chair Professor of the Division of Social Science, delivered to both in-person participants and thousands of online viewers.

Prof. Li Ping highlighted the unprecedented opportunities that artificial intelligence presents for the humanities and social sciences. Citing the recent Dean’s Forum on “Challenges and Opportunities for Humanities and Social Sciences in the Era of AI,” successfully organized by the School, Professor Li emphasized the growing importance of mastering digital humanities methodologies and AI technologies. He encouraged all participants to embrace new technologies with an open mind, examine them with a critical perspective, and engage them with humanistic insight. He also extended a warm welcome to scholars and experts from around the world to visit HKUST.

Prof. Cameron CAMPBELL reflected on over four decades of experience in quantitative historical analysis and digital humanities accumulated by the Lee-Campbell Research Group, which includes Emeritus Prof. James LEE and Prof. CAMPBELL, and other members from international partners. The team has contributed numerous well-known datasets based on primary sources to the academic community. He urged participants to employ computational humanities tools with rigor and a critical approach.

The opening ceremony also showcased a series of cutting-edge research outcomes in digital humanities. Prof. Michael Yan Hon CHUNG, Assistant Professor of the Division of Humanities, unveiled a newly trained Optical Character Recognition model for the Manchu script. This work not only demonstrates the latest AI achievements of HKUST’s digital humanities community but also serves as a model for research on low-resource and endangered languages.

--- 中文 ---

香港科技大學（HKUST）與南京師範大學、澳門大學、北京師範大學—香港浸會大學聯合國際學院及南京農業大學合作，於南京成功舉辦第四屆「語料庫與數碼人文暑期學院」（CDH Summer 2026）。開幕禮由人文社會科學學院院長、社會科學部講座教授李平教授致辭，社會科學部講座教授康文林教授發表主題演講，現場參加者及數千名線上觀眾同步參與。

李平教授指出，人工智能為人文與社會科學帶來前所未有的機遇。他引用學院近期成功舉辦的院長論壇「人工智能時代人文與社會科學的挑戰與機遇」，強調掌握數碼人文方法與人工智能技術日益重要，並鼓勵參與者以開放態度接納新技術、以批判視角審視技術、以人文洞見運用技術，同時歡迎世界各地學者專家到訪科大。

康文林教授回顧李—康研究團隊四十多年來在量化歷史分析與數碼人文領域的經驗。團隊以原始史料為基礎，向學術界貢獻了大量知名資料集。他勉勵參與者以嚴謹與批判的態度運用計算人文工具。
