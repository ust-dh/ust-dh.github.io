**HKADH 2027 | Model/Making: Toward Generative Humanities** — the annual conference of the Hong Kong Association for Digital Humanities — will take place **21–24 January 2027** and is now accepting paper, panel, and poster submissions.

## Theme

This year's conference, *Models as Methods, Media, and Engines of Cultural Production*, asks how computational models — from statistical corpora to large language models — have become central to the way culture is analysed, produced, and circulated.

## Submission guidelines

- Papers: 20-minute presentations on original research
- Panels: three to four papers around a shared theme
- Posters: work-in-progress and early-stage projects
- Lightning talks: five-minute introductions of tools, datasets, or demos

## Important dates

- **Abstract deadline:** 15 October 2026
- **Notification of acceptance:** mid-November 2026
- **Conference dates:** 21–24 January 2027

**HKADH 2027 | Model/Making: Toward Generative Humanities** — 香港數碼人文學會年度會議 — 將於**2027年1月21至24日**舉行，現正徵收論文、專題小組及海報投稿。

## 會議主題

本屆會議以*「模型作為方法、媒介與文化生產引擎」*為主題，探討計算模型 — 從統計語料庫到大語言模型 — 如何成為文化分析、生產與流通的核心。

## 徵稿類別

- 論文：20分鐘原創研究報告
- 專題小組：圍繞共同主題的三至四篇論文
- 海報：進行中及早期階段的研究項目
- 閃電演講：五分鐘介紹工具、數據集或示範

## 重要日期

- **摘要截止：** 2026年10月15日
- **錄取通知：** 2026年11月中旬
- **會議日期：** 2027年1月21至24日
