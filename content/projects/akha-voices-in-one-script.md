The Akha oral tradition is vast, but every researcher wrote it down differently. This project brings those spellings into one script — and the texts into English and Chinese.

--- 中文 ---

阿卡口傳傳統浩瀚，但每位研究者記錄下來的拼寫都不一樣。本計劃把這些拼寫收攏到同一套文字，並把文本譯成英文與中文。
