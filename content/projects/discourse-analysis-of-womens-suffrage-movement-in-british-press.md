The British press argued about women's suffrage for a century. This project reads 1,150 articles and readers' letters as one corpus, and watches the language move.

--- 中文 ---

英國報刊爭論婦女參政權，爭了一百年。這個計劃把 1,150 篇報道與讀者來信當成同一份語料來讀，看語言怎樣一步步移動。
