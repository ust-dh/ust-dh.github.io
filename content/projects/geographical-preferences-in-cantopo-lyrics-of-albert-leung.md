Albert Leung wrote more than a thousand Cantopop lyrics. This project reads every place name in them, and finds that two places do almost all of the work.

--- 中文 ---

林夕寫過一千多首粵語歌詞。這個計劃把歌詞裡每一處地名讀出來，結果發現：整張地圖幾乎只由兩個地方撐起。
