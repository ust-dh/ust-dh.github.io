For four centuries European mapmakers drew a region they had never seen. This project turns that archive of guesswork into a searchable, annotated digital atlas.

--- 中文 ---

四百年間，歐洲製圖師一直描繪一片他們從未踏足的土地。本計劃把這批充滿推測的檔案，變成可檢索、可註釋的數碼地圖集。
