Fifty-eight wall labels from the M+ Museum, read as a corpus. The museum writes to welcome, not to instruct, and the numbers say so.

--- 中文 ---

把 M+ 博物館五十八張展籤當成一份語料來讀。這間博物館用的語氣是「歡迎」，不是「教導」：數字也這樣說。
