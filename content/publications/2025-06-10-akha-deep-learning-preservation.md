## Abstract

Akha, a Tibeto-Burman language spoken across Southeast Asia, faces serious preservation challenges. This paper reports the **world's first deep learning application for Akha**, developed to support automatic speech recognition, script conversion, and machine translation.

## Approach

- Community-driven data collection and annotation
- Transfer learning from related languages to overcome scarce training data
- A reusable pipeline for other under-resourced indigenous languages

## 摘要

阿卡語是東南亞多國使用的藏緬語系語言,正面臨嚴重的保存挑戰。本文報告**全球首個阿卡語深度學習應用**,支援自動語音識別、文字轉換與機器翻譯。

## 研究方法

- 以社區為主導的數據收集與標註
- 從相關語言遷移學習,克服訓練數據匱乏的問題
- 可應用於其他資源匱乏的原住民語言的可重用流程
