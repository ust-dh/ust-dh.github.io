## Abstract

This paper reports the construction of the **BCC diachronic corpus**, a large-scale resource documenting the evolution of contemporary Chinese over the past century.

## Contributions

- Corpus architecture balancing diachronic coverage, genre balance, and computational access
- Methodological discussion of building diachronic language resources
- Case studies on lexical and grammatical change, informed by corpus statistics and digital humanities methods
