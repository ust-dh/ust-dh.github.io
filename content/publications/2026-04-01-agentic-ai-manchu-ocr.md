## Abstract

Manchu OCR remains notoriously difficult because of the vertical script and mixed Manchu–Chinese layouts in historical documents. This paper moves beyond fine-tuned recognition models toward **agentic workflows**, in which AI agents orchestrate multiple tools — segmentation, recognition, post-correction, and evaluation — to progressively refine transcriptions.

## Findings

- Agentic pipelines outperform single-model baselines on degraded 18th–19th century manuscripts
- Iterative post-correction reduces character error rates by a substantial margin
- The workflow is reusable for other low-resource historical scripts

## 摘要

滿文OCR因直排文字與滿漢混排的歷史文獻而格外困難。本文超越單一的微調識別模型,提出**智能代理工作流**,由人工智能代理協調文字分割、識別、後期校正與評估等多種工具,逐步完善轉錄結果。

## 主要發現

- 智能代理流程在品質欠佳的18至19世紀手稿上優於單模型基準
- 迭代式後期校正大幅降低字符錯誤率
- 該工作流可應用於其他資源匱乏的歷史文字
