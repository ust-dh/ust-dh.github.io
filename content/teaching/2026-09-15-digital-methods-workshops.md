The **Open Digital Methods Workshops** run throughout the academic year, offering hands-on, no-prior-knowledge sessions for students, faculty, and staff.

## Sample sessions

- Web scraping 101 (no-code and Python editions)
- Data cleaning with no-code tools
- Data visualization with Tableau
- Fine-tuning LLMs for humanities research
- Digital storytelling and interactive maps

## Format

Sessions are 2 hours, in person or online, and open to the whole HKUST community. Watch the Events page for upcoming dates.

--- 中文 ---

**數碼方法公開工作坊**於整個學年舉行,為學生、教師及職員提供毋須預備知識的實作環節。

## 部分主題

- 網絡數據擷取入門(免編程版及 Python 版)
- 以免編程工具整理數據
- 以 Tableau 進行數據視覺化
- 為人文研究微調大語言模型
- 數碼敍事與互動地圖

## 形式

每節兩小時,設現場及網上場次,向科大全校開放。最新日期請留意「活動」頁面。
