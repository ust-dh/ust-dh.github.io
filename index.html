About us

The Humanities and Digital Technologies Platform is a collaborative community aimed at bringing together faculty, library, and students to explore digital humanities skills and develop innovative digital projects. It bridges the gap between science and the humanities through digital methods and practices.

Launched in spring 2022 by the Division of Humanities and the HKUST Library, the platform supports and promotes digital humanities work across the university — coordinating training for students and research assistants, publishing and disseminating digital projects produced by HKUST students and faculty, and collaborating with regional partners through the Hong Kong Digital Humanities consortium series of lectures and workshops.
