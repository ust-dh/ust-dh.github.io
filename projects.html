Projects

We support a range of digital humanities infrastructure and, through experiment and exploration, move the humanities forward.

HDT CoLab

HDT CoLab explores the development and application of digital tools for research and teaching in the humanities, and opens those tools to the whole community.

Internet RadioEmbedded SystemsMedia LiteracyFall 2026

AI Passport Radio: a self-contained radio for young listeners

Internet-radio firmware for the FoloToy AI Passport, an ESP32-C3 wearable with a screen and three buttons. It carries 49 curated stations from Hong Kong, Singapore and Malaysia and plays them straight from the broadcaster, with no phone and no browser in the loop.

Read more →
Virtual CurationMuseum StudiesSpatial DesignFall 2026

Curate Studio: a block-by-block virtual curation studio

A teaching platform on which a class builds an exhibition block by block: the teacher opens a gallery and sets permissions, the students raise the walls, hang the works and write the labels, and visitors walk through the published show.

Read more →
OpenStreetMapCollaborative MappingUrban DataFall 2026

OSM City Online: a multiplayer OpenStreetMap editor

A multiplayer map editor whose server holds its own mirror of the real OpenStreetMap data for a city. Everyone who signs in edits the same vector map at the same time, and the result exports as a standard .osm file.

DHI Seed Fund Projects

Seed-funded research projects that build digital methods, tools and collections for the humanities.

Language PreservationSpeech TechnologyCorpus BuildingFall 2025

Akha Voices in One Script: A Project of Digitalization and Standardization of Oral Texts

This seed project digitizes Akha oral and ritual texts held in four fieldworkers' archives, converting older, idiosyncratic scripts into the standardized Common Akha Orthography and exploring translation software for English and Chinese.

Read more →
Historical CartographyGIS AnalysisCentral AsiaSpring 2026

Mapping Tartary: A Digital Exploration of European Cartography

A 2026 DHI seed project led by Dr. Marco CABOARA that builds a digital platform for European printed maps of Tartary from the 16th to the 18th century, with GIS analysis and teaching resources.

Read more →
Digital EditionTextual ParallelsHan StudiesMar–Jul 2026

A Hyperlinked Edition of the Huainanzi

This seed project is building a colour-coded, hyperlinked digital edition of the early Han scripture Huainanzi, making its textual parallels with pre-Han writings searchable, comparable and citable. The first two chapters are complete, with the full text targeted by 2029.

Read more →
Medicinal AnimalsModern ChinaStorytelling MapSpring 2026

Medicinal Animals and Digital Storytelling Map: The Story of the Saiga Antelope, Rhinoceros, and Shark

A 2026 DHI seed project led by Prof. Liz Pui Yee CHEE that maps how the saiga antelope, rhinoceros and shark became medicine and food across 20th-century East and Southeast Asia.

Read more →
OCRMachine TranslationManchu StudiesFall 2025

Manchu OCR and Translation AI

This 2025 Fall seed project is building a high-accuracy system that automatically reads and segments Manchu script from historical archives, using a fine-tuned vision-language model trained on synthetic and real scans; the next phase adds transliteration and machine translation.

Digital Humanities Teaching Support Projects

Course-based projects by HKUST students, produced with the platform's digital humanities teaching programme.

Discourse AnalysisDigital HistoryGender StudiesFall 2025

Discourse Analysis of Women’s Suffrage Movement in British Press (Pre-1928)

A Fall 2025 student project for HUMA5630 that applies corpus linguistics and computational text analysis to British newspaper coverage and readers’ letters in Gale, tracing how public discourse on women’s suffrage shifted between 1815 and 1929.

Read more →
Data ExtractionScholarly NetworksScientometricsFall 2025

Tracing Scholars’ Digital Identities: An Automatic System for Multisource Verification and Extraction

A HUMA5630 Digital Humanities group project that builds an automated system to verify and extract scholars’ digital identities from Web of Science, Google Scholar and Scopus, using the CUSD-CPEA database of 1,831 China–US PhD programme alumni to resolve name ambiguity.

Read more →
Named Entity RecognitionLiterary StudiesMachine LearningFall 2025

The Application of NER Models in the Literature Field

A HUMA5630 Digital Humanities student project that builds Chinese NER models — CRF, BERT-Tiny and BERT-Base — for modern and contemporary literature, comparing their accuracy and F1 scores and showing how entity recognition can support geocriticism.

Read more →
Cantopop LyricsText MiningCultural GeographyFall 2025

Geographical Preferences in Cantopo Lyrics of Albert Leung (林夕)

This HUMA5630 student project applies named entity recognition to more than 1,000 Cantopop lyrics by Albert Leung, examining how he maps emotion onto places and contrasting Japan as distant travel with Hong Kong as home.

Read more →
Sentiment AnalysisMuseum StudiesWord FrequencyFall 2025

Reading the Emotions of the Museum: A Sentiment and Word-Frequency Analysis of M+ Exhibit Labels

A HUMA5630 student project that analyses 58 Traditional Chinese exhibit labels from the M+ Museum with word-frequency, word-cloud and sentiment tools, finding a strongly positive, visitor-centred tone in the museum's language.
