HUMA5630 — Digital Humanities Projects

A project-based course in which students design and build digital humanities projects, from interactive maps to AI-assisted archives.

Term
Fall 2026
Level
Postgraduate Course
Date
Sep 1, 2026

HUMA5630 Digital Humanities Projects is a project-based postgraduate course that takes students from research questions to working digital humanities outputs.

Course outline

  • Digital methods and tools: mapping, text mining, web archiving, storytelling
  • Project design: data, audience, and sustainability
  • Building a team project with mentorship from faculty and librarians
  • Presenting and publishing the finished project

Past highlights

Student projects have included interactive historical maps, digital storytelling platforms, OCR pipelines for historical scripts, and AI-assisted translation tools — many of which are showcased on this site.
